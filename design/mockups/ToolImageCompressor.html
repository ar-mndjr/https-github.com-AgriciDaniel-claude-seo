Why compress images for SEO?

Images are usually the heaviest part of a page. Oversized images slow loading, hurt Largest Contentful Paint and push mobile visitors away before the page appears. Compression keeps them sharp while cutting file size.

How to use the image compressor

  1. Drop up to 20 images into the box.
  2. Adjust quality if needed. 75–85% suits most photos.
  3. Compare before and after sizes.
  4. Download the files and replace the originals on your site.

Image optimization checklist

  • Resize first: no wider than the largest display size.
  • Use modern formats such as WebP or AVIF.
  • Lazy-load images below the fold.
  • Write descriptive alt text for every meaningful image.

Image Compressor FAQs

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs in your browser.

Will compression make images blurry?

Not at sensible settings. Around 80% quality is usually indistinguishable from the original.

What is a good image size for the web?

Most images should be under 200 KB; hero images under about 300 KB.
