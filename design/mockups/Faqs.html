About SEO

How long does SEO take to work?

Most websites see early ranking movement in 3–4 months and meaningful traffic growth in 6–12 months. Technical and on-page fixes often show results within weeks.

Can anyone guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Google controls rankings. SEOCampaign commits to the work, transparent reporting and goals agreed up front.

Is SEO still worth it with AI search?

Yes. AI answers draw heavily on well-ranking, well-structured sites.

What's the difference between technical and on-page SEO?

Technical SEO covers how your site works; on-page SEO covers what's on each page.
