What is a SERP simulator?

A SERP simulator shows how a page's title tag, URL and meta description will look in Google's results. Google truncates by pixel width, not character count, so a preview is the most reliable check.

How to use the SERP simulator

  1. Enter your page title and meta description.
  2. Add the URL to preview the breadcrumb.
  3. Switch between desktop and mobile.
  4. Adjust until both fit, then copy the tags.

How to write a snippet that gets clicks

  • Lead with the keyword near the start of the title.
  • Stay within length: about 50–60 characters for titles, 150–160 for descriptions.
  • Give a reason to click: a benefit, number or promise.
  • Be unique on every page.

SERP simulator FAQs

Why does Google rewrite title tags?

Google may rewrite titles that are too long, vague or don't match the page.

Does the meta description affect rankings?

Not directly, but it can raise click-through rate.

How long should a title tag be?

Around 50–60 characters, or under about 600 pixels.
