What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a proposed standard: a plain Markdown file at the root of your site that summarizes who you are and links to your most useful pages. It gives AI assistants a fast, clean way to understand your content without parsing menus, scripts and ads.

How to use the llms.txt generator

  1. Add your brand name and a one-line summary.
  2. Import pages from your sitemap or add them by hand.
  3. Group pages into sections such as Services and Guides.
  4. Write a short description for each link.
  5. Upload the file to yoursite.com/llms.txt.

Tips for AI search visibility

  • Lead with your best pages, not every URL.
  • Describe each link plainly so AI tools know when to cite it.
  • Keep it current when you launch or retire pages.
  • Pair it with schema markup and a clear robots.txt.

llms.txt Generator FAQs

Do AI tools actually read llms.txt?

Support is growing but not universal. It is a low-effort way to make your site easier for AI tools to understand.

Is llms.txt the same as robots.txt?

No. robots.txt controls crawler access. llms.txt describes and links your key content.

Will llms.txt improve Google rankings?

No direct effect on Google rankings. It supports visibility in AI assistants.
