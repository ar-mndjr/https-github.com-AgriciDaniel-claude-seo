Which image format is best for SEO?

WebP is the safe default for the web: much smaller than JPG and PNG and supported everywhere. AVIF compresses even further and suits large photos. Keep PNG for images that need sharp edges and transparency, such as logos and screenshots.

How to use the image converter

  4. Replace the originals on your site and update any links.

Format quick guide

  • WebP: best all-round choice for photos and graphics.
  • AVIF: smallest files for large photos.
  • JPG: fallback for older systems and email.
  • PNG: logos, icons and screenshots with transparency.

Image Converter FAQs

Does converting to WebP help rankings?

Indirectly. Faster pages improve Core Web Vitals and user experience, which support rankings.

Will WordPress accept WebP?

Yes. WordPress supports WebP uploads natively, and AVIF in recent versions.

Is quality lost when converting?

Very little at default settings, and usually nothing visible.
