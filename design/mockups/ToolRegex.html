Why use regex for SEO?

Regular expressions let you filter thousands of queries or URLs at once. In Search Console you can isolate question keywords, separate brand from non-brand traffic, or group pages by folder. In redirects, one rule can handle an entire section.

How to use the regex tester

  1. Enter your pattern.
  2. Pick the flavor: Search Console, GA4 or server redirects.
  3. Paste sample queries or URLs.
  4. Check the highlighted matches, then copy the pattern into your tool.

Useful SEO regex patterns

  • Question queries: ^(how|what|why|when|where|who)\b
  • Non-brand queries: use "Doesn't match regex" with your brand name.
  • Long-tail queries: ^(\S+\s){4,}\S+$
  • Blog URLs: /blog/.*

Regex Tester FAQs

Does Search Console support regex?

Yes. Query and page filters accept RE2 syntax, which does not support lookaheads.

Is regex case sensitive?

In Search Console, add (?i) at the start to ignore case.

Can a regex mistake break redirects?

Yes. Always test patterns before deploying redirect rules.
