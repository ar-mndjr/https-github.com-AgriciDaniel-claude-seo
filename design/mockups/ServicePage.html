Signs your website has technical SEO problems

  • New pages take weeks to appear in Google, or never do.
  • Search Console shows "Crawled – currently not indexed".
  • Traffic dropped after a redesign or migration.
  • Your site is slow on mobile or fails Core Web Vitals.
  • Key JavaScript content doesn't appear in Google's rendered view.
