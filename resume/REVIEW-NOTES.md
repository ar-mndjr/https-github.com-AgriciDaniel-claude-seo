## 5. Which file to send
- `.docx` - use this for ATS applications (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, SEEK, LinkedIn Easy Apply).
- `.txt` - use for plain-text paste fields and older ATS forms.
- `.md` - the master copy. Edit here, then regenerate the other two.
- Export a PDF from the `.docx` only when a job ad explicitly asks for PDF.

## 6. ATS formatting choices made here
Single column, no tables, no text boxes, no headers or footers, no images or icons, no
columns. Calibri throughout, standard section headings, real bulleted list items, contact
details in the document body rather than a header, and dates as "Month YYYY - Month YYYY"
with a plain hyphen.
