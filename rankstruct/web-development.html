Web development FAQs

1. How much does a website cost?

It depends on the platform, number of pages and features. Simple Wix or Squarespace sites cost the least, while custom Next.js builds cost the most. Every project gets a fixed price before work starts. Book a free call for a quote within 48 hours.

2. How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites launch in 2–6 weeks. Simple sites can be ready sooner, while large ecommerce or custom builds may take longer. Your scope will include exact dates.

3. Which is better: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or Wix?

It depends on your goals. Shopify is best for online stores, WordPress for content-heavy sites, Webflow for design-led marketing sites and Wix for simple small-business sites. We'll recommend the right fit on our consultation call.

4. Can you customize my existing website?

Yes. We add features, redesign pages, fix speed issues and connect integrations on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix and custom sites, without rebuilding from scratch.

5. Can you move my website to a different platform?

Yes. We migrate sites between any platforms and set up redirects so you keep your Google rankings and traffic.

6. Will my new website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site includes technical SEO foundations: fast load times, clean URLs, meta tags, schema markup, sitemaps and analytics. As an SEO agency, we build sites that are ready to rank from day one.

7. Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. We offer ongoing support for updates, security, backups and small changes, so your site stays fast and secure.

8. Will I own my website?

Yes. You own 100% of the website, code, content and accounts from day one.
