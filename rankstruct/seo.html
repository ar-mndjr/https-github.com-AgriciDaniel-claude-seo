// New: GEO

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): get cited by AI.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your brand visible and cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. When someone asks an AI tool “What's the best [your service]?”, GEO helps make sure your business is part of the answer.

What our GEO service includes:

AI Visibility Audit

We test hundreds of real buyer questions across major AI platforms to see where your brand appears, where competitors appear instead and why.

Citation-Worthy Content

We restructure your content with clear facts, original data, expert quotes and direct answers, the formats AI models prefer to cite.

Entity & Brand Optimization

We strengthen how AI understands your brand, using consistent information across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Wikipedia/Wikidata and industry directories.

Brand Mentions Across the Web

AI models learn from the whole internet. We grow your mentions on trusted sites, review platforms, Reddit, YouTube and industry publications.

AI Crawler Access

We make sure AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot can reach your content, and that key pages render without relying on JavaScript.

AI Visibility Tracking

Monthly reports showing how often your brand is mentioned and cited in AI answers, compared with competitors.

// New: AEO

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): be the answer.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) makes your content the direct answer to your customers' questions, in featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, voice assistants and AI answer boxes. Instead of just ranking, you become the source that gets quoted.

How we do it:

Question Research

We find the exact questions your buyers ask, from Google, Reddit, forums, sales calls and AI prompts.

Answer-First Content

Clear, 40–60 word answers at the top of each section, followed by detail. That structure is easy for search engines and AI to lift and quote.

FAQ & HowTo Structure

Well-organized FAQ sections, step-by-step guides, comparison tables and definitions that match how answers are displayed.

Schema Markup

Structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Product, Organization, Article) that helps search engines understand your content and show it in rich results.

Voice Search Optimization

Natural, conversational answers optimized for Siri, Alexa and Google Assistant queries.

SEO FAQs

Clear answers on how modern SEO, GEO and AEO work.

1. What are SEO services?

SEO services improve your website's visibility in search engines so more of the right people find you. They include technical fixes, content, link building and local SEO. Our SEO services also cover AI search through GEO and AEO.

2. What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the process of getting your brand mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini. It focuses on authority, clear facts, brand mentions and content that AI models trust.

3. What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO optimizes your content to be the direct answer to a question, in featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, voice assistants and AI answers. It uses clear, question-focused content and structured data.

4. Is SEO still worth it with AI search?

Yes. AI tools rely heavily on well-ranked, trusted web content to generate answers. Strong SEO is the foundation for AI visibility. Businesses that combine SEO with GEO and AEO are best placed to win.

5. How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Content and authority usually take 3–6 months to build meaningful growth, depending on your competition and starting point.

6. Can you guarantee #1 rankings or AI citations?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. No one controls Google or AI models. We guarantee clear work, transparent reporting and proven methods that consistently grow visibility.

7. How do you measure AI visibility?

We regularly run a set of real buyer questions across major AI platforms and track how often your brand is mentioned, cited or recommended, compared with your competitors.

8. Do you offer local SEO?

Yes. We optimize your Google Business Profile, local listings, reviews and location pages to help you rank in the map pack and local AI answers.
