The person behind RankStruct

Hi, I'm April.

My journey into digital work started while I was studying BS Computer Engineering. During that time, I began doing freelance web development as a side hustle. Building websites introduced me to the technical side of helping businesses establish their presence online.

That led to a question that changed the direction of my work:
How do people actually find these websites?

I became curious about how search engines evaluate websites and what makes a page visible in search results. I started learning SEO through free resources, paid masterclasses, hands-on experimentation, and real client projects.

As I worked with different businesses, I learned that SEO is more than rankings. A strong digital presence depends on how a website is structured, how information is presented, how users find what they need, and how different systems work together.

That experience naturally expanded my work from SEO and web development into automation and digital systems.

I continue to develop my skills through hands-on projects, ongoing research, technical learning, and staying current with changes in search, web technologies, automation, and AI. Working with businesses also means every project brings a different challenge, which creates opportunities to learn, test, and improve.

That continuous learning is part of how RankStruct operates.

I bring together SEO, web development, and automation to help businesses solve practical digital problems with solutions that fit their goals, requirements, budget, and timeline.
